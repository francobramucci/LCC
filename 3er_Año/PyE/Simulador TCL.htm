How to draw your own distribution

×

Using the mouse, click anywhere inside the individual distribution graph and draw while holding down the mouse button. (You don't need to use the pencil and you don't need to drag the line that is already there.)


When you lift the mouse button, the program will re-scale the graph so that the histogram for each sample will better fit into the window. It will still be the same distribution that you drew.
